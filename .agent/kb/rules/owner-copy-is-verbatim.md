# Build with the owner's words as given

When the owner gives you copy, such as a headline, a label or a call to action, use those exact
words. If you think other words work better, say so in one line with the reason and ask. Keep the
owner's words in every draft until the owner picks yours by name.

**Why:** on 2026-11-04 the owner asked for a homepage cue reading "Scroll down to see why". I
suggested "How it works" alongside the layout, the owner said "yes go", and I built two drafts with
my words. The owner: "Plus you changed my copy." A "go" on a proposal that bundles a layout with new
wording approves the layout, not the rewrite.

**How to apply:**
- Quote the owner's copy word for word in the task's acceptance criteria, so a later agent can't
  drift from it.
- Ask a separate question about wording ("keep 'X', or use 'Y' because …?"). Never fold a wording
  change into a design proposal.
- This covers site, docs and CLI text the owner dictates, and figures they asked to be phrased a
  certain way (see [[website]] for the recorded ones).
