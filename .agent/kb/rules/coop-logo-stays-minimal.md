# Keep the Coop logo minimal and in Coop colors

Use the approved Contained entrance artwork: detached roof, enclosed round opening,
amber structure and colon, thin cyan entrance rim, ivory lettering and graphite ground.
Use the outlined wordmark rather than retyping it. Keep the same geometry across the
website, README, icons, social cards and sibling product cards.

The owner rejected literal chickens, eggs, ramps, fences and sandbox illustrations as
irrelevant detail. Suggest isolation through the enclosure and negative space. Do not add
locks or shields. The Emisar/Ryker relationship comes from simple geometry; their mint
does not replace Coop's amber/cyan branding.

Inspect the native vector against the approved raster and review small exports and actual
page placement. In sibling product tiles, keep the visible Coop symbol near 63% of
the square canvas width to match the inset Ryker and Emisar marks. The concept raster's
texture and label are not production artwork.

In website navigation, use a 28.8 × 25.6px symbol beside the 77.2 × 22.5px wordmark
on desktop, with smaller responsive sizes. Align the visible bottom of the symbol with
the full wordmark bottom, including the p descender. Center the combined row within
the link target. Account for the mark's 20-unit bottom padding in its 320-unit SVG canvas.
